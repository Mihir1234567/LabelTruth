Scientific Methodology, Mathematical Formulation & Ethical Framework

Theoretical architecture behind the LabelTruth project, the international NOVA classification system, and statistical inference on corporate health-washing.

1. The NOVA Food Classification Framework

Proposed in 2009 by Prof. Carlos Monteiro and the Center for Epidemiological Studies in Health and Nutrition at the University of São Paulo, NOVA classifies foods according to the extent and purpose of industrial processing rather than in terms of nutrients alone.

  • NOVA 1 (Unprocessed / Minimally Processed): Biological parts of plants or animals subjected to physical cleaning, freezing, or drying without chemical modifications.
  • NOVA 2 (Culinary Ingredients): Pure extracts (oils, sugars, salts, starches) used to prepare culinary dishes.
  • NOVA 3 (Processed Foods): Whole foods combined with culinary ingredients (salt, oil, sugar) to prolong preservation.
  • NOVA 4 (Ultra-Processed Foods): Formulations made primarily of industrial substances (emulsifiers, colorants, hydrogenated oils, high-fructose corn syrup).
2. Mathematical Modeling & Explainable AI

Natural language ingredient listings are tokenized and mapped into a sparse 2,000-dimensional vector space using Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency (TF-IDF) with unigram and bigram n-grams:

$\text{TF-IDF}(t, d) = \text{TF}(t, d) \times \log\left(\frac{1 + n}{1 + \text{DF}(t)}\right) + 1$

Multinomial Logistic Regression models class log-odds linearly: $\log\left(\frac{P(Y=k)}{P(Y=K)}\right) = \beta_{0, k} + \sum_{j=1}^p \beta_{j, k} x_j$. Because the transformation is strictly linear, the exact feature weight $\beta_{j, k}$ provides uncompromised mathematical attribution.

3. Addressing The Accuracy Paradox & Class Imbalance

Because NOVA 4 represents 67.09% and NOVA 2 represents only 0.75% of products in Open Food Facts, standard loss functions starve minority gradients, producing an deceptive 95.19% overall accuracy while recall for NOVA 2 drops to 4.0%.

We resolve this by implementing cost-sensitive inverse-frequency sample weighting: $w_j = \frac{N}{K \cdot N_j}$. This boosts Balanced Accuracy to 81.85% and recovers NOVA 2 recall to 58.7%.

4. Objective B: The Health-Washing Hypothesis

We formalize the marketing claim vs. processing test via Pearson's Chi-Square Test of Independence ($\chi^2 = 409.96$, $p < 10^{-80}$). While statistically dependent, the effect size measured via Cramér's $V$ is only 0.0905.

This indicates that in practice, front-of-package marketing claims (e.g. 'Natural', 'High Protein') are completely decoupled from genuine minimal processing, as 53.67% of products carrying claims are ultra-processed industrial formulations.
